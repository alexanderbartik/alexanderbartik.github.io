Advising

I enjoy advising PhD students and working with them to develop their research ideas. Please use this link to schedule meetings with me. I'm happy to have one-off meetings or a more intensive schedule. For students that become one of my advisees, I prefer more frequent, shorter meetings to infrequent longer meetings (i.e. I'd rather meet once every week or two for 5-10 minutes each than once every two months for 45 minutes). I give all my advice with free disposal.

I'm proud of the students I am currently advising and have advised before, see links to their work below.
